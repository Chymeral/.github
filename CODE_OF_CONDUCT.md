# Chymeral Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Chymeral to be a constructive, respectful, and technically rigorous
project for everyone who participates in it, regardless of experience, identity,
background, or preferred programming language.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, code, and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Give technical feedback that is specific, respectful, and actionable.
- Welcome good-faith questions and acknowledge uncertainty or mistakes.
- Respect differing use cases, platforms, accessibility needs, and language
  ecosystems.
- Keep public discussion appropriate for a collaborative open-source project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal insults.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, reviews, or issue tracking.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Bad-faith misrepresentation of another person's work or position.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict
participation, or take other proportionate action when behavior conflicts with
this code. Decisions are made to protect the project and its participants, not
to suppress technical disagreement expressed respectfully.

## Reporting

Do not report a conduct concern in a public issue or pull request. Contact a
Chymeral organization owner privately through GitHub, state that the report is
confidential, and include the relevant repository, date, participants, and a
concise description of the concern. Maintainers will handle reports with as much
privacy as the circumstances permit.

## Scope

This code applies to Chymeral repositories, issue trackers, review discussions,
release discussions, and other spaces managed by the Chymeral organization.
